Where the capital sits

The map is the easy part. The capital stack is the deal.

Hyperscale and AI campuses are being delivered through structures that look nothing like real estate: off-balance-sheet joint ventures, residual value guarantees, project bonds sold against a single lease, and outright platform buyouts by long-term asset owners. This map plots known US projects by where they sit and how much capital they carry — Meta in blue, everyone else colored by lifecycle. Click any node to open the deal and see its capital stack, terms, and counterparties.

Platform capital & long-term owners

Not every commitment attaches to a single site. These are the pools, platforms, and asset owners funding the build-out — the counterparties a hyperscaler negotiates with, and the institutions that end up owning the powered land and delivery teams after a platform is bought.

What this is

A working map of how large-scale US data center capacity is being financed and owned, built to compare structures rather than count buildings. Assembled from public sources — not an investment recommendation.

Confidence marking

Filed — company release or securities filing. Announced — named-party press release. Reported — press citing unnamed sources; terms may move.

Known gaps

General contractors, GMP terms, and fund LP identities are disclosed inconsistently. Those fields are left blank rather than inferred. Marker size uses announced project or transaction value where available.
